Full Name: Elena Koval
Date of Birth: March 14, 1988
Profession: Senior Interior Designer & Spatial Strategist

Career Overview:
Elena Rostova is an acclaimed interior designer with over 12 years of professional experience specializing in sustainable residential architecture, luxury commercial interiors, and adaptive space reuse. 

Early Career & Education:
Elena graduated with distinction from the Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) in 2010 with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Interior Architecture. She began her career as a Junior Designer at Studio Moderno in New York, where she contributed to high-profile residential renovations in Manhattan and Brooklyn, developing a deep appreciation for minimalist aesthetics paired with warm, tactile materials.

Mid-Career Development & Innovation:
In 2015, Elena relocated to San Francisco to join Vanguard Spatial Design as a Senior Associate. There, she spearheaded the firm's commercial workplace initiative, designing ergonomic, flexible office layouts for leading tech startups. Her signature approach integrates biophilic design principles—incorporating natural light, indoor foliage, and organic textures—to improve occupant well-being and environmental sustainability.

Current Practice:
In 2021, Elena founded her independent design practice, Rostova Design Lab. Her studio focuses on net-zero residential spaces and boutique hospitality interiors. Her recent projects include the award-winning renovation of a historic eco-lodge in Sonoma Valley and several luxury smart-home apartments across the West Coast.

Key Skills & Expertise:
- Conceptual Design & Spatial Planning
- Sustainable & Eco-Friendly Material Selection
- Custom Furniture & Lighting Integration
- Project Management & Contract Administration
- Advanced 3D Rendering (SketchUp, AutoCAD, Revit, Rhino)
